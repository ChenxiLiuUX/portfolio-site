Design system v1.1

Portfolio design system

Space Mono and Plus Jakarta Sans with a vermilion accent. Every value on the site comes from the tokens in styles.css, so a change made there updates every page at once.

Principles

The work is the color

The site stays neutral so project images, with their own palettes, are always the loudest thing on the page. Vermilion is for interaction and status only.

Reading comes first

Case studies are mostly writing. Body text is large, lines stay short, and every heading is scannable on its own.

Structure you can see

Thin rules and square corners give the site its quiet, slightly brutalist frame. No shadows, gradients, or rounded cards.

Color

Twelve colors, including two for the dark About panel. Text colors all pass WCAG AA on both backgrounds, and most pass AAA. The bright signal orange is never used as text.

Container

Split layout

Every section head uses a 1:2 split, with the heading on the left and reading text on the right. It stacks below 600px.

Breakpoints

900px (summary and metrics reflow) and 600px (single column, smaller type and spacing).

Rules and corners

Ink rules (1px) frame cards, tables, and section tops. Line hairlines divide inside a block. Corners are always square.

Motion

Color and underline changes only, 180ms. Turned off entirely for people who prefer reduced motion.

Components

Usage rules

Do

Use tokens for every color, size, and space. Keep vermilion to links, status, hover states, and quote rules. Write captions that tell people what to notice.

Don't

Add new colors per page, use the signal orange as text, round corners, add shadows, or animate anything beyond color and underline.
